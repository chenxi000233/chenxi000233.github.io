About Me

Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science, Wuhan University, advised by Prof. Xu Chen.

My primary research focuses on cross-modal retrieval and multimodal representation learning for remote sensing. I also work on image segmentation, scene text detection, and LLM-based AI agents.

  • Remote Sensing Retrieval: image-text retrieval, efficient retrieval, continual retrieval, and video-text retrieval.
  • Computer Vision: image segmentation and scene text detection in challenging visual conditions.
  • AI Agents: LLM-based financial analysis and tool-using research workflows.

I am open to research discussion and collaboration.

Research Focus

Primary Research Theme

Remote Sensing Cross-Modal Retrieval

My work studies how remote sensing images and videos can be aligned with natural language, progressing from fine-grained image-text matching toward efficient retrieval, continual retrieval, and event-centric video-text retrieval.
